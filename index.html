Update Log

v0.2.4
  • Fixed cross-network connections: removed a built-in relay server that had been shut down for months (it could never actually help, only ever the shared backup relay was doing any work)
  • Relay Server menu now points you to a free personal TURN account for players who still can't connect — much more reliable than the shared public one
  • Slightly faster connection setup (ICE candidate pre-gathering)
v0.2.3
  • Building & fence collision is now an exact box (not a circle), matching the mesh corner-to-corner — no more clipping into a building's corner or spawning stuck against one
  • City map rebuilt on a real layout: exactly 6 named buildings in the Alley-Ways, each one big block that fully fills its own footprint
  • Map is bigger and wider overall, cleanly split into two halves — Park on the left, Alley-Ways on the right
  • Wider, less cramped lanes between buildings and along the border
  • Border wall of buildings is fully sealed all the way around — no gaps to slip through or get stuck in
v0.2.2
  • City map is now a wide rectangle instead of a square, with real Alley-Ways (narrow building-lined lanes) instead of open lots
  • Nearly 3x the buildings, filling out the extra width on both sides of the road
  • The map border is now an actual ring of buildings you can see and bump into, not an invisible wall
  • Park is enclosed by a proper post-and-rail fence instead of floating hedge boxes
  • Trash bags and dumpsters look like actual trash now (lumpy bags, tied tops, spilled litter) instead of plain dark blobs
v0.2.1
  • Removed the arrow that pointed straight at the chaser — use buildings and the park to break line of sight and loop them
  • City map rebuilt: Alley-Ways (buildings, dumpsters, trash bags, fences) and a fenced Park (trees, a path) split by a road, on a tighter map
v0.1.2.1
  • More intense LMS: distorted, faster theme that ramps up as time runs out
  • LMS rounds are capped at 30s and ability cooldowns are 40% shorter
  • Heartbeat red vignette, camera shake and a bigger VS intro
v0.1.2
  • Painter puddles slow everyone else (Slowness 2) while they stand in them
  • Freeze now lasts 5s; Piggy Bank keeps the gore and adds scattered coins
  • Chase theme gets louder as you get close to the chaser; LMS theme + VS banner at 1v1
  • New footstep sfx (walk, run, mud/paint) plus freeze, cage, decoy, coin and panting sounds
v0.1.1.9
  • New potato skins: Piggy Bank (explodes into coins), Mutated (different every time), Hologram
  • Push now shoves everyone away from you
  • New player abilities: Decoy (35s) and Freeze (40s)
  • New chaser ability: Cage (30s)
v0.1.1.8
  • Taunt button: plays a looping emote until you move
  • Customize: pick 1 of 3 taunt emotes (Dance, Wave, Spin) and a potato skin (Default, Bomb, Dynamite, Tomato)
  • Relay Server menu: use your own TURN relay, or force relay-only connections
v0.1.1.7
  • Effect pop-ups on the right (name, level, time left) e.g. “Speed 2 - 4s”; running animation is now clearly different from walking
  • New player abilities: Energy Drink, Painter, Builder (15s walls only Players can pass), Push
  • New chaser abilities: Screech, Decoy. Cards are now random Player + Chaser combos
v0.1.1.6
  • New connecting screen when joining a lobby: “Finding lobby”, then “Connected to lobby, now connecting”, with a Cancel button
  • Crossplay: joining works across different networks and long distances (relay fallback incl. TCP/443 for strict firewalls)
  • Clearer join errors (lobby not found, blocked network, timeout); brief matchmaking hiccups no longer kick you
